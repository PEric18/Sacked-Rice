Who we are

Sacked is a small rice shop with a simple belief: rice deserves the same care as coffee or wine. Most of us grab whatever's on special, yet the world grows thousands of rices, each with its own aroma, texture and story. We seek out the ones worth cooking, from fragrant Thai Hom Mali to Sri Lankan red rice and Spanish Bomba, and bag them up for Australian kitchens. Every rice comes with where it's grown, how it cooks and what to make with it, so you can match the grain to the dish. A small range, chosen for quality and taste.
